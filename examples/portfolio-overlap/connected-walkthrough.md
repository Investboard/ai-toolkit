# Rebalance with new money before selling

**Connected workflow preview.** Prepared ahead of native rebalance MCP
integration. The current connector cannot generate this comparison yet.
[Try the file-only version now](walkthrough.md#try-it).

The intended experience reads your own permitted holdings and mandate, uses
Investboard's deterministic class-level calculation, explains alternatives
against your rules and drafts the reason you explicitly choose. Reuse an
existing connection; no new permissions or connection are authorized by this
document. [Choose your assistant](https://investboard.com/product/ai-toolkit#install).

The worked case is illustrative, not a customer account or a connected transcript.
Both sample funds are equities. The 90/10 equity/cash targets contain no separate
MSCI World or DAX target. [Inspect the sourced comparison](README.md#compare-four-ways-to-proceed).


## 1. Read portfolio and plan together

> Read my authorized holdings and mandate for the same scope. Show asset-class allocation, target bands, reserve and evidence dates. Identify missing evidence or permissions. Do not save a check or change records.

## 2. Compare new money with alternatives

> I have €400 of new investable money and prefer buying underweighted classes before selling. Use Investboard's rebalance comparison on the same snapshot. Compare unchanged, €400 without sales, €100 without sales, no-contribution rebalancing and a €100 hybrid. Show class-level changes, cash, weights, drift and cost/tax coverage. Do not save. If the comparison tool is unavailable, stop and say so.

## 3. Check what each route achieves

> Compare these routes with my own bands, reserve and preference. Distinguish exact targets from within-band status, and show the minimum contribution for a no-sale solution before costs. Do not invent fund targets, fees, tax estimates, execution prices or a requirement to trade. Do not save a check.

## 4. Keep the reason for the choice

> I select the €400 contribution-only candidate for further review. Draft my reason, alternatives and remaining checks. Acknowledge that the unchanged portfolio also fits my bands. Do not infer execution, later performance or a reminder, and do not save a journal review.

## What a good result establishes

At €11,306.20, the sample is 91.16% equities / 8.84% cash, within its bands.
€400 makes equities underweight before purchases: buy €229.38 of equities and
retain €170.62 cash to restore 90/10 gross. With €100 and no sales, keep the
whole contribution in cash for 90.36/9.64%, still partial. The minimum new money
for exact, unrounded targets is €145.14 before costs.

Without new money, selling €130.62 of equities restores targets gross; with
€100, the required gross sale falls to €40.62. The investor prefers the €400
route because the cash is available and avoiding sales matters to them. That
is not a forecast or a universal ranking.

No fund is selected for trading. No executable quotes, cost basis, fees, minimum
trade sizes, share lots or fractional dealing support have been supplied, so
execution and after-cost feasibility remain unverified. A draft is not a saved
journal review. Current check/outcome tools save only when separately and
explicitly requested; a saved outcome does not prove execution.
